I am passionate about continuously developing my technical skills and staying informed about the evolving challenges and technologies within the energy industry. I thrive in multidisciplinary environments, enjoy working collaboratively, and am committed to continuous learning and delivering practical, data-driven solutions.

I aspire to build my career in seismic interpretation and geomechanics within the energy industry, contributing to safer, more efficient, and better-informed subsurface exploration and development.

Undergraduate Thesis
Pore Pressure Prediction and Overpressure Zone Characterization in the Poseidon Field, Browse Basin, Australia using Eaton and Kan & Swan Methods through Well Log and Seismic Data Integration

This research focuses on predicting pore pressure and delineating overpressure zones by integrating well log and seismic data, strengthening my understanding of pore pressure prediction, geomechanics, rock physics, and seismic interpretation.

Thesis Overview

This study integrates well log and seismic data to predict pore pressure and delineate overpressure zones in the Poseidon Field, Browse Basin, Australia, using Eaton and Kan & Swan methods. Accurate pore pressure prediction is critical for safe and efficient drilling operations, as inaccurate estimates can lead to drilling hazards such as kicks, blowouts, and wellbore instability, as well as significant time and cost losses. In addition, effective well planning requires understanding the lateral distribution of pore pressure, not just relying on measurements from existing wells. To address this, my research integrated well-based pore pressure estimation with seismic-derived Vp and density volumes from simultaneous seismic inversion to characterize and map overpressure zones across the Poseidon Field. I also compared the Eaton and Kan & Swan methods to assess the consistency and reliability of their pore pressure predictions after well-level calibration. This research has strengthened my interest in drilling engineering, wellbore geomechanics, and mud engineering, particularly in understanding how subsurface pressure and rock properties influence drilling performance and wellbore stability. I am also still deeply interested in seismic interpretation and rock physics, especially in how seismic and well data integration can be used to better characterize pore pressure and support safer, more efficient drilling decisions.The work strengthened my understanding of pore pressure prediction, geomechanics, rock physics, seismic interpretation, and drilling-related subsurface challenges.
